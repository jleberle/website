Here's a relatively extensive semi-list of the things I read and listen to on a regular basis. Anything digital that has a plus after it is something I actively pay for.

## Print Media

Increasingly it feels like the best option is a return to reading things off line and away from screens. A result I've subscribed to a handful of magazines that show up in the mail on a weekly, monthly, or quarterly basis:

- [*Commonweal*](https://www.commonwealmagazine.org): The liberal Catholic magazine. Provides some good non-religious
  articles and reviews and provides a helpful connection when you grew up Catholic.
- [*Dissent*](https://dissentmagazine.org): Quarterly political commentary and reviews. Shows up as a nice
  bound publication.
- [*The Economist*](https://www.economist.com): Leans a bit too heavily into the economics naturally but it
  does provide the best weekly overview of the world. 
- [*New York Times*](https://nytimes.com) (Sunday Edition): Say what you will about the paper, it's
  still a lovely experience to sit down with the paper on Sunday morning.
- [*n+1*](https://www.nplusonemag.com): Quarterly everything publication. Shows up in an even beefier bound
  publication. 

## Local News

It still makes me sad to think about the *Tulsa World* [limping along](https://jaredeberle.org/articles/i-got-sick-of-watching-the-tulsa-world-die/) but there are a number of good local media sources in Tulsa:

- [*The Frontier](https://www.readfrontier.org)
- [*NonDoc*](https://nondoc.com)
- [*Oklahoma Voice*](https://oklahomavoice.com)
- [*Oklahoma Watch*](https://oklahomawatch.org)
- [*The Pickup*](https://thepickup.com/grieving-city-devastated-by-loss-of-iconic-public-art-installation)
- [*Tulsa Flyer*](https://tulsaflyer.org)

I also donate to [KWGS](https://www.publicradiotulsa.org) and [OETA](https://oeta.tv), our local NPR and PBS stations.

## Traditional Non-Print Media

The great thing about your local public library is they usually provide any number of magazine subscriptions and my local library offers subscriptions through Libby and Press Reader. As a result I can read a number of periodicals and newspapers for free. I don't dip into these as much but I do technically "subscribe" to them.

- *[The New Yorker*](https://www.newyorker.com)
- [*High Country News*](https://hcn.org)
- [*New York Review of Books*](https://nybooks.com)
- [*London Review of Books*](https://lrb.co.uk)

I also regularly read [*The Atlantic*](https://theatlantic.com) online but I'm not subscribed to it. It's not traditional but I will add [*Defector*](https://defector.com)+, the sports website that grew out of the death of *Deadspin*.

## Newsletters

I've trimmed down the number of newsletters I subscribe to through [Feedbin](https://feedbin.com) but they still make up the bulk of my digital readings. I now tend to focus more on newsletters that provide summaries and digests as a way of minimizing my reading time online but still getting a broad idea of what's happening.

### Internet

- [*Garbage Day*](https://www.garbageday.email)+: How I know what's happening online now that I don't do social media.
- [*Installer*](https://www.theverge.com/installer-newsletter): Tech recommendations every Saturday morning.
- [*Links I Would Gchat You If We Were Friends*](https://linksiwouldgchatyou.substack.com)
- [*Read Max*](https://www.patreon.com/cw/MaxRead)+: Mostly subscribe for the weekly movie and book recommendations.
- [*Today in Tabs*](https://www.todayintabs.com)+: Not as often as it used to be, but also helpful for online
  trends.
### Sports

- [*The Athletic's* newsletters](https://www.nytimes.com/athletic/5803046/2025/12/31/the-athletic-newsletters-sign-up/): Currently the daily Pulse, F1, hockey, and
  college football.
- [*Cup of Coffee*](https://www.cupofcoffeenews.com)+: Baseball (and other stuff)
- [*Daniel Benson's Cycling Substack*](https://dnlbenson.substack.com)+: More info than I will ever need on cycling
- [*The Guardian's Football Daily*](https://www.theguardian.com/football/series/footballdaily): Still wish it was called The Fiver
- [*The Outer Line*](https://theouterline.substack.com/subscribe): Weekly cycling recap
- [*Sports! With Rodger Sherman*](http://sports.beehiiv.com): A little bit of everything.

### Miscellaneous/Commentary

- [*Will Leitch*](https://williamfleitch.substack.com): Could go under sports but writes enough about life to go here.
- [*Craig Mod*](https://craigmod.com): Walking, Japan, and photography.
- [*John Ganz*](https://www.unpopularfront.news)+: Political commentary
- [*Field Notes from Christopher Brown*](https://fieldnotes.christopherbrown.com): Urban nature dispatches
- [*Robin Sloan*](https://www.robinsloan.com): A bit of everything and olive oil.

## Podcasts

I tend to listen to podcasts on the way to work, so these tend to fall off in listening during the summer. These are all available anywhere you can find podcasts.

### Politics

- [*Know Your Enemy*](https://dissentmagazine.org/category/podcast/know-your-enemy/)+: Mostly focused on the modern conservative movement
- [*Unclear and Present Danger*](https://jamellebouie.net/unclear-and-present-danger)+: Jamelle Bouie and John Ganz's podcast on 90s
  political thrillers, the Patreon is more political
- [*In Bed With the Right*](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/in-bed-with-the-right/id1696774612): Gender and social politics 

### News

- [*Panic World*](https://www.garbageday.email/panic-world): Companion podcast to *Garbage Day*
- [*Reuters World News*](https://www.reuters.com/podcasts/reuters-world-news/): 10 minute summary every morning.
- [*Search Engine*](https://www.searchengine.show): Exploring any number of stories.

### Sports

- [*The Distraction*](https://defector.com/the-distraction-podcast): Everything and anything
- [*Eye on College Basketball*](https://www.cbssports.com/podcasts/eye-on-college-basketball/): College Basketball
- [*Guardian Football Weekly*](https://www.theguardian.com/football/series/footballweekly): Soccer
- [*Puck Soup*](https://www.pucksoup.com): NHL
- [*Shift+F1*](https://www.f1.cool): Formula 1
- [*Shutdown Fullcast*](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/shutdown-fullcast/id738433869)+: College football

### Everything Else

- [*Desert Oracle Radio*](https://www.desertoracle.com/radio/): Dispatches from the Mojave Desert
- [*The Talk Show*](https://daringfireball.net/thetalkshow/): John Gruber's Apple Podcast
- [*Lore*](https://www.lorepodcast.com) and [*Cabinet of Curiosities*](https://www.grimandmild.com/curiosities): Aaron Mahnke's weird histories
