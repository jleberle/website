Here's a relatively extensive semi-list of the things I read and listen to on a regular basis.

## Print Media

Increasingly it feels like the best option is a return to reading things off line and away from screens. A result I've subscribed to a handful of magazines that show up in the mail on a weekly, monthly, or quarterly basis:

- *New York Times* (Sunday Edition): Say what you will about the paper, it's
  still a lovely experience to sit down with the paper on Sunday morning.
- *The Economist*: Leans a bit too heavily into the economics naturally but it
  does provide the best weekly overview of the world. 
- *Commonweal*: The liberal Catholic magazine. Provides some good non-religious
  articles and reviews and provides a helpful connection when you grew up Catholic.
- *Dissent*: Quarterly political commentary and reviews. Shows up as a nice
  bound publication.
- *n+1*: Quarterly everything publication. Shows up in an even beefier bound
  publication. 

## Local News

It still makes me sad to think about the *Tulsa World* [limping along](https://jaredeberle.org/articles/i-got-sick-of-watching-the-tulsa-world-die/) but there are a number of good local media sources in Tulsa:

- *Tulsa Flyer* 
- *NonDoc*
- *Oklahoma Voice*
- *The Frontier*
- *Oklahoma Watch*
- *The Pickup*

I also donate to KWGS and OETA, our local NPR and PBS stations.

## Traditional Non-Print Media

The great thing about your local public library is they usually provide any number of magazine subscriptions and my local library offers subscriptions through Libby and Press Reader. As a result I can read a number of periodicals and newspapers for free. I don't dip into these as much but I do technically "subscribe" to them.

- *The New Yorker*
- *High Country News*
- *New York Review of Books*
- *London Review of Books*

I also regularly read *The Atlantic* online but I'm not subscribed to it. It's not traditional but I will add *Defector*, the sports website that grew out of the death of *Deadspin*.

## Newsletters

I've trimmed down the number of newsletters I subscribe to through [Feedbin](https://feedbin.com) but they still make up the bulk of my digital readings. I now tend to focus more on newsletters that provide summaries and digests as a way of minimizing my reading time online but still getting a broad idea of what's happening.

### Internet

- *Garbage Day*: How I know what's happening online now that I don't do social media.
- *Today in Tabs*: Not as often as it used to be, but also helpful for online
  trends.
- *Links I Would Gchat You If We Were Friends*: Third internet related
  newsletter.
- *Read Max*: Another internet focused newsletter, mostly subscribe for the
  weekly movie and book recommendations.
- *Installer*: Tech recommendations every Saturday morning.

### Sports

- *Cup of Coffee*: Baseball (and other stuff)
- *Sports! With Rodger Sherman*: A little bit of everything.
- *The Guardian's Football Daily*: Still wish it was called The Fiver
- *The Outer Line*: Weekly cycling recap
- *Daniel Benson's Cycling Substack*: More info than I will ever need on cycling
- *The Athletic's* newsletters: Currently the daily Pulse, F1, hockey, and
  college football.

### Miscellaneous/Commentary

- *Will Leitch*: Could go under sports but writes enough about life to go here.
- *Craig Mod*: Walking, Japan, and photography.
- *John Ganz*: Political commentary
- *Field Notes from Christopher Brown*: Urban nature dispatches
- *Robin Sloan*: A bit of everything and olive oil.

## Podcasts

I tend to listen to podcasts on the way to work, so these tend to fall off in listening during the summer:

### Politics

- *Know Your Enemy*: Mostly focused on the modern conservative movement
- *Unclear and Present Danger*: Jamelle Bouie and John Ganz's podcast on 90s
  political thrillers, the Patreon is more political
- *In Bed With the Right*: Gender and social politics 

### News

- *Panic World*: Companion podcast to *Garbage Day*
- *Reuters World News*: 10 minute summary every morning.
- *Search Engine*: Exploring any number of stories.

### Sports

- *Shutdown Fullcast*: College football
- *Shift+F1*: Formula 1
- *Puck Soup*: NHL
- *Guardian Football Weekly*: Soccer
- *Eye on College Basketball*: College Basketball
- *The Distraction*: Everything and anything

### Everything Else

- *The Talk Show*: John Gruber's Apple Podcast
- *Desert Oracle Radio*: Dispatches from the Mojave Desert
- *Lore* and *Cabinet of Curiosities*: Aaron Mahnke's weird histories
