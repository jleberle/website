210 pages released in May 2020 in response to a Freedom of Information Act
request filed on 1 November 2017, in three files: the Bureau's investigation of
the February 1973 protest at the Custer County Court, a 1976–77 extremist
investigation conducted while Banks was teaching at DQ University in California,
and the closing of that investigation after the California Supreme Court held he
could not be extradited to South Dakota. The scanned files are published on this
site with the writing that cites them.
