Rodeo is often seen as a white, conservative sport within Modern America. These posts grew out of a digital history course in 2015 that sought to complicate our understanding of rodeo by looking at Native American participation in the spot. What these posts show is rodeo has been a traditional source of community for Indigenous communities and a source of identity in the same way as it is in white communities throughout the west. At the same time it remains a deeply complicated sport that reminds us that sports are never just about sports.
