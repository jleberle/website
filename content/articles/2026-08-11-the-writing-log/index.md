I think it's helpful to document the writing amounts you do on a regular basis, even better if it's public. I don't truly believe anyone will meaningfully look at the [writing log](https://jaredeberle.org/writing-log/#months) I've built and email me when I stop doing stuff, but it's a helpful motivating factor regardless.
<!-- more -->
This is just a quick post to document the fact it exists and note what it does. Essentially it pulls in material I write for the blog and within my private on-going projects. It only pulls from specific project folders within Obsidian and the public facing blog posts in Articles and Reviews on the site. When building the count it takes a census of the data to prevent duplication and removes blockquotes and other material that isn't directly my writing. It's not completely perfect but it provides me a rough idea of the writing I've done. 

When I'm done with writing for the day I can run `site logwriting` and it will build out my writing, add it to the database and ship it to the site. The script hashes paths so that no private documents leak out onto Github when it's pushed to the repo. 

Again, not perfect or exact but a good guideline for what I've done and a helpful addition to my weekly, monthly, and yearly recaps so I can see what I did (or did not do) over the various periods.
